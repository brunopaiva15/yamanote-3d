Yamanote 3D - 山手線

One loop. Thirty stations. No objective.

A contemplative browser ride on Tokyo's Yamanote Line: you are a passenger on a JR East E235 series train, going round the thirty-station loop in near real time - about sixty-seven minutes a lap. Nothing to win, nothing to score. Walk the carriage, sit down, watch the city drift past, listen to the announcements and the departure melodies.

On board

  • Walk the carriage in first person, sit down on a seat, or hold a strap.
  • Step off through an open door and stay on the platform: the train leaves without you, and the next one comes about two minutes later.
  • Hear announcements and 発車メロディ departure melodies in Japanese and English for all thirty stations and both directions - 内回り and 外回り.
  • Pick the date and time before boarding: the season colours the trees, the sun follows the hour, and the weather changes the journey.
  • Talk to fellow passengers. The interface is available in English, French, and Japanese.

How it was made

Getting this close to the real line took more than 50 hours of documentary research alone - timetables and running times, station and platform layouts, E235 series interiors and displays, announcement scripts, departure melodies - and more than 100 hours of work in total, even with AI helping. Every detail on board was checked against a source before it was built.
